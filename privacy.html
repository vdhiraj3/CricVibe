🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At CricVibe Portal, accessible from our official website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by CricVibe Portal and how we use it.

1. Log Files and Analytical Metrics

CricVibe Portal follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies Configuration

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve programmatic advertisements to our site visitors based upon their previous digital visits across standard web networks. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the official Google advertising structures.

3. Embedded Content and External Links

Our layout utilizes embedded structures (iframes) to display live automated cricket match trackers and data schedules. These third-party components operate under their respective parental network policies. We explicitly advise our tracking users to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers or data providers for more detailed digital guidance.

4. Children's Information Safeguard

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. CricVibe Portal does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

5. Consent Affirmation

By using our custom utility structures and live score tracking boards, you hereby consent to our corporate Privacy Policy and absolutely agree to its programmatic terms and configurations.
